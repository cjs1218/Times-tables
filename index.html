      Trickiest facts right now

        Practice away from the screen

        • Say the story for the fact of the week in the car or at bedtime.
        • Count in 3s, 4s or 6s while climbing stairs or bouncing a ball.
        • Ask one tricky fact a day, then ask it again tomorrow.
        • Let your child teach you a trick. Explaining it helps them remember.
        • Short and often beats long and rare: 5 minutes a day is plenty.
